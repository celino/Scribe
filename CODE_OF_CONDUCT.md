# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project and we want people to feel welcome to report a bug, ask a question or
send a patch, whatever their experience.

**Expected:** respectful, patient communication; criticism of the code and the ideas, not the person; giving credit;
saying "I don't know" or "I was wrong".

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and
pressure on the maintainer or other people to act faster.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and PRs that break these rules, and may block people who do it
again. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how). It will be handled
with discretion.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
